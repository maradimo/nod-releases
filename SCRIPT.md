If you'd rather cut to real footage of Nod there, each part holds an 11 second slot.
- The ask (scene 8) is a statement, not a request, because you are not yet raising. If you want a direct line for YC or speedrun ("I'd like to build this in the batch"), tell me the exact wording and I'll put it in both the script and the animation.
- Scene 1's long prompt is illustrative of the problem, not a quote from a user.
- The 90 second cut felt slow. This one runs 61 seconds: every transition is under a third of a second, text lands at half-second intervals, and each demo beat is five seconds. The voiceover is about 165 words, which fits 60 seconds at 2.8 words a second.
