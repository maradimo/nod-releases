Install Nod

A menu-bar app for the Mac. It watches what you are doing and has your next message ready before you ask. Tap ⌘ on its own and the words are typed for you.

Download Nod for Mac

You need an Apple Silicon Mac on macOS 14 or newer, and a brain for Nod: either 16 GB of memory for the local model, or an Anthropic API key. The first run sets up whichever you pick.

First run, about ten minutes

  1. Open the zip and drag Nod.app to Applications. Then open it. If macOS says it cannot verify the developer, open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway next to Nod, and open it again. This happens once.
  2. Pick where Nod thinks. "A model on this Mac" needs Ollama; Nod checks for it and pulls the model (about 5 GB) with a progress bar. Nothing you type or read leaves your Mac. Or paste a Claude API key; it goes into your Keychain and nowhere else.
  3. Two permissions. Accessibility lets Nod read the field you are typing in; Screen Recording lets it read the window with Apple's on-device text recognition. Grant both from the buttons in the window. Microphone and Speech are asked for only if you use the voice features.
  4. The tutorial. One minute: you try each gesture for real.

Using it

  • A small pill sits in a corner. When Nod has a guess, the pill names it in one line and gets out of your way; tap ⌘ to open the card with the words ready, and ⌘ again to do it.
  • ⌘ tap = yes. ⌥ tap = next idea, or not now. Hold ⌘ = all ideas. Hold ⌥ = tell it what you want, by voice or typing. esc closes the card.
  • In a chat with an assistant or a person, type half a sentence, pause, tap ⌘: Nod finishes it in your words.
  • The first days it works from what is on your screen. After that it starts to know what you decided, prefer and have to do, and the cards get specific.

What it keeps, and where

  • Everything is redacted before it is stored: passwords are never read, card numbers, keys and tokens are masked, apps like password managers contribute only their name.
  • Menu bar › What Nod knows shows what it learned about you, with the source of each item, and lets you forget any of it.
  • Menu bar › What left this Mac today shows every payload sent to the model you chose. With the local model, that list is empty.
  • A nightly encrypted backup goes to your own iCloud Drive, if you have one. Nothing goes to us.
  • During the beta we ask you to turn on Share usage (last step of the first run, or the menu bar): once a day Nod sends what it did and how long it took, cards, taps, timings, errors, never what you read or wrote. Menu bar › Show the last usage report shows exactly what goes out; the switch turns it off any time.

When something is wrong

Menu bar › Send the log saves a redacted log (no screen text, no messages) to Downloads and opens an email. A sentence about what you expected is the most useful thing you can add.

Stopping and removing

Menu bar › Quit Nod stops it. To remove it, drag Nod.app to the Trash and delete ~/Library/Application Support/Nod.
